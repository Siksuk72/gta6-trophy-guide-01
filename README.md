A complete trophy guide for **Grand Theft Auto VI** on PlayStation 5 — all **55 official trophies** (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), each with a step-by-step guide in plain language: what to do, where to go, how long it takes, whether it can be missed, and what to watch out for. No copied text, no fake specifics — just honest guidance built from the official trophy list.

Follow Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval across the state of Leonida — from the streets of Vice City and Ocean Beach to Vice City International Airport and the swamps beyond — and work your way from the opening mission, *Gone Fishing*, to the platinum, *Vice Got a Price!*

## How to run

No build step, no dependencies. Just open the guide:

- **Simplest:** open `app/index.html` in any browser.
- **Or serve it locally:** run `node serve.ts` and open the address it prints.

## Features

- **Every trophy, fully guided** — all 55 trophies with original 80–150 word guides, difficulty ratings (1–5), time estimates, missable flags, and trophy kind (story, collectible, activity, combat, exploration, money, social).
- **Honest about the unknown** — where the official description is all that is known, the guide says so and flags the uncertainty instead of inventing fake details.
- **Counts where they matter** — 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 100 vehicles, 100 WAiNK locations, 3 card sets, 8 stranger strands, 50 challenges, 20 races, 5 bank vaults, 10 registered vehicles, and more.
- **Hidden trophies marked** — the 28 story and secret trophies hidden on PlayStation until earned are flagged so you can avoid spoilers or chase them deliberately.
- **Sources cited** — every trophy lists its sources, including the official trophy lists and Rockstar Games.
- **Search and filter** — find trophies by name, grade, kind, difficulty, or missable status.

## Data

Trophy names, descriptions, and grades are the official list as of 2026-11-18. All guides are original writing based on the official descriptions and publicly known facts about GTA VI. The full dataset lives in `app/data/trophies.json`.
